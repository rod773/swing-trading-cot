Create a TradingView Pine Script indicator called cot.pine using the formula from the attached video transcript (1-.srt). The video describes Larry Williams' 1998 WillVal valuation method:

Formula:
1. Take weekly data
2. Calculate a 2-week WMA of the data series
3. Calculate a 22-week WMA of the data series
4. diff = shortMA - longMA
5. Normalize diff as a percentage of its 3-year rolling range: (diff - min) / (max - min) * 100

Interpretation: 0-100 scale. High = overvalued/overbought, Low = undervalued/oversold. Same formula structure used for COT report analysis (as mentioned at subtitle 53-54).
