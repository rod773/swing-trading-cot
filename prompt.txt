Modify cot.pine so that the data used is the spread of Gold and the symbol of the chart where the indicator is being used.

Formula:
1. Fetch Gold price via configurable ticker (default XAUUSD)
2. Get the chart symbol's close
3. spread = goldClose - chartClose
4. Calculate a 2-week WMA of the spread
5. Calculate a 22-week WMA of the spread
6. diff = shortMA - longMA
7. Normalize diff as a percentage of its 3-year rolling range: (diff - min) / (max - min) * 100

Interpretation: 0-100 scale. High = overvalued/overbought, Low = undervalued/oversold.

The indicator should:
- Let the user configure the Gold ticker
- Let the user configure MA periods and lookback
- Plot the %-rank with overbought/oversold reference lines at 80/20
- Include overbought/oversold background fills
